BowlByte

Intelligent canteen automation, smart queues, and food waste records

Team: [not recorded] · Guide: [not recorded] · College: [not confirmed in the repository]

Problem

Counters guess readiness, can promise the last portion twice, and rarely keep an audit of cash or discarded food.

Proposal

Students order in the browser. The kitchen works by station. Cashiers record cash and scan a one-time QR. Managers record production, waste, and a forecast they must approve.

Architecture

Next.js and FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery. The browser stays on one origin. Money and stock change only inside database transactions.

Modules

  • Accounts and canteen roles
  • Menu, reservation, kitchen ETA
  • Cash receipts and QR pickup
  • Unclaimed-order hold
  • Forecast and preparation advice

Evidence

Automated tests cover ordering, cash races, and one-time pickup. On a seeded series, seasonal naive validation MAE was 2.143 and was kept over gradient boosting at 3.000.

Conclusion

Ready for a local demonstration and a supervised counter if a roster is supplied. Not a public site. Phone notifications were not observed. No waste reduction is claimed.
